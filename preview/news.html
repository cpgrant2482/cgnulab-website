KamLAND searches for diffuse supernova neutrinos

Using the full KamLAND dataset, the collaboration reported a search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background that employs a neural network to classify events and reduce backgrounds.

Eos reports water-phase detector performance

The Eos collaboration published measurements of its detector performance with water, building toward future studies of hybrid Cherenkov and scintillation detection.

KamLAND-Zen publishes its complete-dataset search

The collaboration reported its search for Majorana neutrinos using the complete KamLAND-Zen dataset, with major contributions from U.S. researchers to analysis and interpretation.
